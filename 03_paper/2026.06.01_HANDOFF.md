## Contamination bug found in the ORIGINAL pipeline (important)
`01_build/03_pull_ibes.R` pulls actuals WITHOUT a `pdicity='QTR'` filter, so for ~88 fiscal-year-end names the match grabbed the **annual** actual instead of quarterly (e.g. GS $51.32 annual vs $14.01 quarterly — both exist in IBES at the same period-end/anndats). Effects on the ORIGINAL paper:
- Walk-down regression intercept **+$1.55 → ≈ 0** (β 1.15 → 1.04, R² 0.675 → 0.98). Qualitative walk-down survives (companies beat by ~5¢ median).
- Flips **28/337 beat/miss outcomes** (beat rate 80.7% → 73.6%), shifting #1/#2/#4 numbers favorably and likely the **$0.53 EPS waterfall**.
- Strategy returns/alpha (#3) **unaffected** (they don't use actual EPS — verified: identical gated by contaminated vs clean actuals).
- Reproduced the paper's exact published coeffs (1.5474/5.88/1.153/0.675) before/after the one-filter fix → diagnosis is solid. WRDS IBES frontier is genuinely 2026-02-19 (verified live, both `ibes` and `tr_ibes` schemas) — NOT a stale-file issue.
