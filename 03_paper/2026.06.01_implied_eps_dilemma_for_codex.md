# Investigate: does the prediction-market "implied EPS" actually beat analyst consensus?

## Background
The paper *Minority Report* (working paper, `03_paper/main.tex`) studies Polymarket earnings beat/miss markets vs IBES analyst consensus on 340 events (Sept 2025–Feb 2026). One of its four contributions (#2) is an **"implied EPS"**: invert the crowd's beat probability `p = P(EPS > line)` into a dollar point estimate and show it is more accurate than the analyst consensus.
- Method A (parametric): `implied_eps = line + sigma * t_inv(p, df=4)`, `sigma` = rolling 12-quarter std of the firm's historical surprises.
- Method B: empirical-CDF inversion of trailing surprises.
- Code: `02_analysis/06_implied_eps.py` → `analysis/implied_eps_results.csv`. Waterfall figure: `02_analysis/04_surprise_decomp.R` (lines ~336–446) → `analysis/fig_eps_waterfall.pdf`.
- **Published claims** (see `main.tex`, section "Implied EPS: From Probability to Point Estimate" and the waterfall caption): full sample n=224, PM-implied MAE $2.415 vs consensus $2.128; **non-GAAP: PM beats consensus by 2.3% (MAE $2.013 vs $2.061, n=139)**; GAAP: PM degrades 37.6% ($3.081 vs $2.239, n=85). Waterfall: median consensus→actual gap **$0.53** = historical bias $0.05 + PM-incremental $0.10 + residual $0.38.

## The data bug that motivates this
`01_build/03_pull_ibes.R` pulls reported actuals from `ibes.actu_epsus`/`ibes.actu_xepsus` selecting `value` **without filtering `pdicity='QTR'`**. IBES stores both a quarterly (`QTR`) and an annual (`ANN`) actual at the *same* fiscal-period-end and announcement date for Q4/fiscal-year-end reporters. The event-level match (nearest `pends` to earnings date) therefore grabbed the **annual** actual for ~88 of 338 events. Example (live IBES): Goldman Sachs, pends 2025-12-31, anndats 2026-01-15 → `QTR` value **14.01**, `ANN` value **51.32**; the market line was 11.49 (quarterly) and the match used 51.32.

Consequences already confirmed (`01_build/2026.06.01_03g_walkdown_clean.R`, which re-pulls actuals with `pdicity='QTR'` and swaps them into the original matched data; output `build/2026.06.01_walkdown_clean.rds` has both contaminated `actual_eps` and clean `qtr_eps`):
- The walk-down regression `actual ~ consensus` goes from the paper's **+1.55 (t=5.88), β=1.15, R²=0.675** to **−0.013 (t=−0.27), β=1.037, R²=0.980** with clean actuals. (Median surprise +$0.05; companies still beat, by pennies.)
- 88/338 events change `actual` by >$1; 28/337 flip beat↔miss; overall beat rate 80.8% → 73.7%.
- WRDS IBES is genuinely current only through 2026-02-19 (verified live, both `ibes` and `tr_ibes` schemas) — not a stale-file artifact.

## The dilemma (what I need investigated)
Recomputing the implied-EPS comparison and waterfall against the **clean quarterly actuals** (`02_analysis/2026.06.01_clean_primary_results.R`) reverses Contribution #2:

| | Paper (contaminated actuals) | Clean (QTR actuals) |
|---|---|---|
| Non-GAAP: implied vs consensus MAE | +2.3% better ($2.013 vs $2.061) | **−2.5% (worse): $0.258 vs $0.252, n=206** |
| GAAP | −37.6% | −220% ($1.414 vs $0.441, n=123) |
| All | — | −113.9% ($0.690 vs $0.323, n=329) |
| Waterfall consensus→actual gap (non-GAAP) | $0.53 | **$0.05** (consensus $1.08 → +bias $0.05 → +PM $0.10 → residual −$0.10 → actual $1.12) |

Note the MAE *levels* also collapse (~$2 → ~$0.25): the paper's ~$2 MAEs were themselves inflated because they were measured against annual actuals. With clean actuals the consensus is already within ~5¢ (median) of the quarterly actual, so there is almost no gap for the crowd's dollar inversion to capture — and Method A adds noise and slightly *overshoots*.

So Contribution #2 flips from "crowd produces a more accurate dollar EPS than analysts" to a **null** (crowd ≈ consensus, slightly worse). The paper's other three findings are unaffected (they use beat/miss probability, order flow, and stock returns — not dollar EPS): calibration/Brier (#1, BSS +0.20 → +0.33 clean), flow→outcome (#2-flow, Q1 beat rate 32.4% → 5.9% clean, logit z≈8.5), short-side return (#3, return robust; FF3 alpha marginal/beta-driven), smart-wallet persistence (#4).

**The decision:** keep the implied-EPS section as an honest null, demote it to a short note, or reframe it as a result about the *limits* of crowd point-estimation. Before deciding, I want the reversal independently pressure-tested.
